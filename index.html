Our Story

Founded in 2015, Sangrila blends contemporary culinary artistry with the warm hospitality of Indian heritage. Our chefs travel globally to source the finest ingredients, turning each plate into a narrative of flavor, texture, and aroma.

Every corner of our dining room is designed to whisper elegance—rich woods, brushed brass, and ambient lighting that frames the culinary performance.
